# How UPLB TKS Helps the University's Personnel

**UPLB Timekeeping System (TKS)** is UPLB's online system for attendance, Daily Time Records (DTR), leave, Certificates of Service (COS), and travel requests. This document explains what it changes for the people who use it every day: employees, timekeepers, signatories, and HR.

---

## At a glance

| Before TKS | With TKS |
|---|---|
| Paper logbooks and bundy cards, transcribed by hand every kinsena | Time-ins are recorded at the moment they happen, through a web browser, a kiosk, a biometric device, or an ID card |
| DTRs typed up manually at the end of each period | DTRs generated automatically in CS Form No. 48 format, one at a time or for a whole office at once |
| Leave forms walked from desk to desk for signatures | Leave filed online, routed to each signatory, with a notification at every step |
| "How many leave credits do I have left?" answered only by asking HR | Leave balances visible to the employee at any time, and matching HRDO's own leave records |
| No way to tell if a printed DTR was altered | Every DTR carries a QR code that anyone can scan to confirm it is genuine |
| Corrections made quietly, with no trail | Every change is logged, and employees can see changes others made to their records |

---

## For every employee

**Clock in without the queue.** Personnel can time in and out from their browser while on an approved campus network, at a kiosk, on a biometric device, or with an RFID card, depending on what their office allows. The time card on the home page shows today's punches and the schedule they are measured against.

**Know where you stand, anytime.** The home page shows:
- today's attendance and a live preview of the current DTR
- remaining leave credits (vacation, sick, and special privilege leave)
- anything waiting for the employee's own signature
- the day's events from their Google Calendar, if they connect it

**File leave in minutes.** Leave applications are filled out online. The system checks eligibility and available credits, routes the form to the right signatories, and sends a notification as it moves. Employees no longer need to follow a form from office to office.

**Get the right schedule.** Regular, flexible, and shift schedules are all supported, including temporary overrides. If an employee's punches consistently match a different shift than the one on file, the system suggests it, and the employee can request the change with one tap.

**Holidays and travel are already counted.** Declared holidays, special non-working days, and filed travel all appear on the DTR automatically. An employee on official travel is not marked absent for it.

**Don't forget to clock out.** Employees can switch on a reminder that sends a phone or browser notification when their shift ends and they are still clocked in.

**See who changed your records.** When HR enables it, each employee gets a read-only list of every change someone else made to their records: attendance a timekeeper added, a DTR that was regenerated, a leave that was signed. They can also see when a timekeeper opened manual entry in their office, for whom, and why.

**Sign in with your UP account.** Personnel sign in with their UP Google account. There is no separate password to remember.

**Help is built in.** Guided tours walk new users through each screen, the Guides page holds step-by-step instructions, and a "What's New" page explains each update in plain language.

**A short break, by choice.** The dashboard includes an optional Break Room mini-game with office leaderboards. It records no attendance data and can be switched off by each person.

## For timekeepers

**One screen for today's presence.** Section Presence shows who in the timekeeper's sections is in, on leave, on travel, or not yet clocked in, for today or any earlier date.

**Generate DTRs in bulk.** A timekeeper can generate DTRs for a whole section at the end of the period and download them as a single ZIP, instead of preparing each one by hand.

**Biometric data merges automatically.** Punches from biometric devices are imported and matched to the right person, then combined with web and kiosk punches into a single record.

**Corrections are tracked.** When a timekeeper adds or fixes an entry, the change is logged with who made it and why. This protects the timekeeper as much as the employee.

**Each timekeeper sees only their own offices.** Timekeepers see only the offices and sections assigned to them, which keeps records private between offices.

**A dedicated handbook.** The *Unit Timekeeper Handbook* (`docs/UNIT_TIMEKEEPER_HANDBOOK.md`) covers every screen, the semi-monthly DTR routine, and how to handle common situations.

## For signatories and supervisors

- A **"Needs your action"** list on the home page gathers every leave application and COS waiting for their signature, each with the button that handles it.
- Leave and COS documents are **signed digitally** within the system and stay on record.
- **Notifications** reach signatories when something lands in their queue, so filings are not left waiting on a desk.

---

## For HR

**University-wide visibility.** Dashboards show office attendance summaries, on-time rate trends, punctuality, pending leave requests, and upcoming holidays across UPLB.

**Leave records that match HRDO's.** HRDO's leave workbook is imported directly into TKS, so the balances employees see, and the leave cards HR prints, come from the same ledger HRDO already maintains. Built-in checks flag rows that look wrong, such as overlapping dates or unknown leave codes.

**Policy changes with a preview.** HR can adjust how leave credits are earned and deducted, and preview how a change would affect balances before saving it.

**One central place for timekeeping rules.** Holidays, leave types, accepted time-in methods, approved campus networks, and timekeeping rules are all managed in one admin panel.

**A full audit trail.** Every create, edit, and delete on important records is logged, and HR can review it.

---

## For the university as a whole

**No double encoding.** Personnel, office, position, and appointment records come automatically from UPLB's HR system. When HR updates someone's appointment, TKS reflects it without anyone re-typing it.

**Trustworthy documents.** Each generated DTR has a QR code and a verification code. Anyone, including offices outside UPLB, can scan the code or enter it on the sign-in page to confirm the DTR is genuine and unaltered.

**Less paper, less waiting.** Leave and COS move through signing online. Fewer forms are printed, carried between buildings, or lost.

**Fair and consistent computation.** Undertime, tardiness, absences, and leave deductions follow the same rules for everyone and use each person's actual schedule. Two timekeepers can no longer reach different answers for the same record.

**Ready for connected systems.** TKS already shares data with UPLB's HR system and the UPLB Performance (IPCR) app. Work is underway to send certified DTR summaries to the payroll system, so that attendance can feed salary computation directly instead of being re-encoded.

**Continuously improved.** TKS has been in active development since June 2024 and is updated frequently in response to feedback from personnel, timekeepers, and HR.

---

## What's still on paper

To be clear about what TKS does not yet do:

- **Travel requests** are filed online, and they already count on the DTR, but signatories still sign the printed travel form by hand. Online travel approval is a planned improvement.
- **Payroll integration** is in progress. DTRs can now be locked once certified, which is the first step toward sending them to payroll.

---

## Summary

TKS replaces manual logbooks, hand-typed DTRs, and paper leave routing with one online system. Employees can see their own attendance and leave credits at any time. Timekeepers spend less time on clerical work and more on checking records. HR gets accurate, university-wide data and a single source of truth for leave. The university gets documents it can verify, and a clear record of who changed what.
